## What we learnt

**About the existing game record.** Topology was **Sensing**; colony 0 played **Default** with 26
live edits, colony 1 was the **Poacher** preset, static. The two presets differ in exactly one atom
(`follow.searching.food.enemy`, 0 vs 3) — an accidental near-controlled A/B. The pool emptied at
t=3210, 41% into the run; colony 1 was functionally dead by t≈1650 with 400 food still on the map.
Nobody fought. It was a commons race, not a war.

**H7 — a lone trace is ignored or it captures everything.** 32 twin runs. `read` and `captured` were
the same event in every single one: 24 runs where no ant ever changed course and the worlds stayed
bit-identical, 8 where 50–100% of the colony diverged permanently. No middle. Direction of effect
was a coin flip (4 helped, 3 hurt) — a false trace **redirects but does not steer**, because the
world adjudicates on arrival.

**H8 — the coordination horizon.** 96 runs. Discovery was **100% at every distance**; sustained
exploitation fell 100% (<30 cells) → 0% (75+). The limit is not on finding, it is on organising
around what was found. Evaporation rate binds only in the middle band (45–59 cells).

**The biggest single thing, found late:** stigsim's `score.ts` computes `Π (pheromone+1)^follow` and
picks by roulette — which **is** the Deneubourg choice function with k=1 and n = the follow weight
(default **5**, biology ≈ 2). The non-linearity is hard-coded, not emergent. This demotes H7 from
discovery to demonstration, reframes H5 (the player was tuning the *choice exponent*, not "trail
fidelity"), and possibly **breaks H3's strong form** — because `evapRate` and *n* look like two
separable knobs, one setting reach and one setting capture probability.

**From the literature critique:** the horizon is a synthesis of limits already known in central
place foraging theory, grid-search bounds and epidemic models; evaporation-as-exploration-dial is
standard ACO; bimodal uptake is the canonical pitchfork bifurcation. **H3 (bound = horizon) is the
one claim identified as novel, and it is still unmeasured.** Adopt "critical region" (Zhong & Evans)
for the small-world argument. Sematectonic stigmergy — where the trace *is* the work, so faking
costs exactly what contributing costs — is the strongest form of the thesis and is not testable in
stigsim.

## Corrections made along the way — do not re-make these

- `topology.provenance` is **not** an ant-facing identity mechanism. Ants never read it; it is the
  researcher's forgery-measurement instrument.
- `tankMax` is the **ant's reservoir**, not a per-cell ceiling. `DenseField.add()` does not clamp.
- `D_max ≈ 1/(8·evapRate)` was derived from one deposit's round trip. The right form uses the
  **passage interval of reinforcing agents**, so colony size belongs in it. Ours underestimated 2–4×.
- Impulse experiments must inject on **quiet ground** (`--where empty`). Injecting on the busiest
  cell adds signal where the gradient already peaks — nothing turns, every run reads as null.
- Define capture by **ants changing course**, not field mass. At slow evaporation the injected blob
  alone stays above threshold all run and fakes a hit. This produced a bogus "8/8" before it was
  caught.
- **3 seeds lied.** The first impulse run at n=3 suggested a smooth band; 5 seeds showed it was
  bimodal; 8 confirmed. Do not report anything below ~8 seeds.
- `results/horizon-tau-ref.json` is **confounded** — source size is entangled with the exploitation
  threshold and the time budget. Kept only as a record of what not to do.
- `pnpm install` had not been run in the stigsim checkout after the update; nothing resolved until
  it was.

## Open / blocked

- **τ_ref is untested and currently untestable.** Needs resources that expire on their own clock,
  independent of consumption. stigsim's war/maze mode does not expose this; `infinite-mode` has
  growth dynamics that might adapt. **This is the most valuable upstream feature request** — it is
  the clock that produces the *hard* horizon.
- The `caut` (cautionary) channel has been zero in every record. Still unknown what writes it.
- Reading list not yet touched: Aswale et al. 2022 first (may already contain E5), then Zhong &
  Evans 2002, Bampas et al. 2023, Perna et al. 2012.
